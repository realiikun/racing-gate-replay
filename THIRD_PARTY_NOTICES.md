# Third-party materials

The labels in inputs/ram_labels.zip are a repackaged six-flight subset of *Race Against the Machine* by TII Racing. The source dataset, including its camera frames and annotations, is available under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). This release selects flights and packages labels for scoring. The detector outputs, replay trajectories, clip-reference coordinates and statistical summaries were generated for the present study. Figure 1 uses crops from the original imagery.

Michael Bosello et al. (2024), *Race Against the Machine: A Fully-Annotated, Open-Design Dataset of Autonomous and Piloted High-Speed Flight*, IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters 9(4), 3799–3806. [Dataset and complete author credit](https://github.com/tii-racing/drone-racing-dataset) · [Dataset paper](https://doi.org/10.1109/LRA.2024.3371288).

Third-party Python packages and separately obtained detector weights retain their respective licenses.
